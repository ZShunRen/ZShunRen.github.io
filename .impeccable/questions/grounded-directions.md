# Grounded directions

1. Blue-hour terrace: open waterfront architecture; blue sky, warm windows, generous typography.
2. Luminous editorial: technology culture magazine; ink-blue pages, white reading text, narrow amber accents.
3. Neon arcade canopy: welcoming entertainment streets; cobalt ground, small coral light sources, broad headings.
4. Optical light installation: museum light art; open dark-blue field, precise luminous arcs, quiet captions.
5. Luminous connections: blue-hour transit wayfinding; fine route lines connect real projects across an open waterfront ground.
6. Architectural model: illuminated future-city exhibition; airy blue plinths, warm windows, carefully spaced labels.
7. Harbor promenade: waterfront public-space graphics; broad horizon, amber reflections, clear directional typography.

The familiar hacker terminal and generic neon dashboard are excluded: both introduce clutter and imply functionality the portfolio does not have. All directions preserve the optimistic city reference and existing content.
